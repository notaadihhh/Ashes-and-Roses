# 🌹 Roses and Ashes

An interactive 3D WebGL packaging showcase built with **Three.js**, featuring custom artwork, dynamic day/night cycles, atmospheric shaders, and switchable environments[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

🔗 **[Launch Live 3D Showcase](https://notaadihhh.github.io/Roses-and-Ashes/)**[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span)

### ✨ Interactive Features
* **Articulated 3D Packaging:** Click or tap the lid to open and close the box, inspect the inner liners, or switch between 5 multi-box display layouts (`Box Position`)[span_7](start_span)[span_7](end_span).
* **Custom Panel Overrides:** Upload your own folder or individual PNG textures (`Panel Images`) to test custom artwork live on the 3D model[span_8](start_span)[span_8](end_span).
* **Switchable Environments:** Explore the model across **Green Meadows** (with animated grass, pond reflections, and fireflies), **Desert Dunes**, the **Moon**, or a **Classic** customizable backdrop[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span).
* **Real-Time Day/Night & Clock:** Drag the 24-hour clock hand, run an automated day/night cycle, or sync the lighting to real-world time[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span).
* **Cinematic Visuals & Audio:** Toggle HDR tone mapping, shaders (atmosphere, gloss, bounce lighting, film grain), magic particles, ambient music, and mechanical lid sound effects[span_11](start_span)[span_11](end_span).
